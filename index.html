Together We Rise

Standing with widows.
Building futures for families.

Ibhade Charity Foundation is dedicated to widows and their children — restoring dignity, building economic independence, and creating pathways to hope through transparent, community-rooted action.

We walk alongside every widow: from grief to empowerment, from survival to thriving.

To provide holistic support to widows and their children through education, economic empowerment, healthcare access, and community reintegration — restoring dignity one family at a time.

A Nigeria where no widow or her children lack dignity, opportunity, or hope — where widowhood is not a sentence but a chapter from which every woman can rise.

Transparency, accountability, community ownership, and verified impact. We act, we prove, and we account for every naira entrusted to us.

Our Programmes

Where your support
creates change.

Six programme areas, each aligned to the UN Sustainable Development Goals — with dedicated field teams, measurable targets, and publicly reported outcomes.

Aligned to the 2030 Agenda for Sustainable Development
Education

Learning Without Barriers

Free learning materials, school rehabilitation, teacher training, and scholarship support for 3,200 children — ensuring inclusive, equitable, quality education for every child.

Edo, Delta & Ondo States · 18 schools
UN SDG Alignment 4 Quality Education
Clean Water

Water for Every Village

Borehole drilling, water point rehabilitation, and community water management training — advancing universal access to safe, affordable drinking water and sanitation for all.

Edo & Delta States · 28 boreholes
UN SDG Alignment 6 Clean Water & Sanitation
Healthcare

Health Within Reach

Free medical outreaches, maternal health support, and community health worker training — ensuring healthy lives and promoting well-being for all ages at every income level.

6 LGAs · 4,800+ patients served
UN SDG Alignment 3 Good Health & Well-being
Empowerment

Widows' Economic Empowerment

Vocational skills training, seed grants, and cooperative formation for widows — advancing gender equality and promoting decent, sustainable livelihoods for women and their families.

Edo, Delta & Ondo · 600+ widows trained
UN SDG Alignment 5 Gender Equality 8 Decent Work
Nutrition

Nourishing the Next Generation

Monthly nutrition support, micronutrient distribution, and community feeding programmes — working towards zero hunger and improved health outcomes for children and nursing mothers.

6 States · 2,400 children reached
UN SDG Alignment 2 Zero Hunger 3 Good Health
Emergency Aid

Crisis Response & Emergency Relief

Rapid deployment of food parcels, shelter support, and psychosocial aid — tackling extreme poverty and reducing inequalities for the most vulnerable widows and families within 72 hours.

Nationwide · 1,800+ families assisted
UN SDG Alignment 1 No Poverty 10 Reduced Inequalities
